Pipeline

From photo to field protocol

Three stages, no manual tuning. The detector runs entirely on the Flask server and streams a fully annotated result back to the browser.

01 / CAPTURE

Upload or capture

Drag a photo in, or stream a frame straight from the device camera. PNG, JPG, BMP and WEBP up to {{ max_upload_mb }} MB.

02 / ANALYSE

Isolate, then infer

OpenCV builds a green-tissue mask with Otsu thresholding to auto-crop the leaf, then YOLOv8 locates every affected region.

03 / ACT

Diagnose and treat

Bounding boxes, confidence scores and the matching treatment protocol for the dominant class are returned as JSON.
